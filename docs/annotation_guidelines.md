# Annotation Guidelines

## Feluda Narrative-Function Annotation

Each sentence in the corpus is assigned one narrative-function category. Three annotators independently label the sentences using the 13-category scheme below.

### 1. Dialogue

A sentence representing direct speech or spoken interaction between characters.

### 2. Narration / Observation

Narrative statements describing events, actions, observations, or general progression of the story that do not belong more specifically to another category.

### 3. Setting / Scene Description

A sentence describing the physical environment, location, surroundings, atmosphere, or scene in which events occur.

### 4. Detective Reasoning / Deduction

A sentence expressing Feluda's or another investigator's reasoning, inference, interpretation, hypothesis, or deduction related to solving the case.

### 5. Travel / Movement

A sentence describing travel, movement from one place to another, transportation, or a journey.

### 6. Character Description

A sentence describing a character's physical appearance, personality, behaviour, habits, or other characteristics.

### 7. Backstory / Exposition

A sentence providing background information, history, context, or explanatory information needed to understand characters, events, or the case.

### 8. Character Introduction

A sentence that introduces or identifies a character, particularly when the character first enters the narrative or becomes relevant to the story.

### 9. Investigation Action

A sentence describing an investigative activity such as searching, questioning, examining, following a lead, collecting information, or taking an action specifically as part of the investigation.

### 10. Crime / Threat / Violence

A sentence describing a crime, criminal activity, threat, attack, violence, danger, or an event directly involving such activity.

### 11. Clue / Evidence

A sentence presenting a clue, piece of evidence, discovery, object, observation, or information that is relevant to solving the mystery.

### 12. Emotional Reaction

A sentence describing a character's emotional response, feeling, fear, surprise, excitement, anger, relief, or similar affective reaction.

### 13. Suspense / Tension

A sentence whose primary narrative function is to create, maintain, or intensify suspense, uncertainty, anticipation, or tension.

## Annotation principle

Annotators assign the category that best represents the **primary narrative function of the sentence in its local context**. When a sentence could plausibly fit more than one category, the label should reflect the function that is most central to the sentence's role in the narrative.

The released CSV preserves the three annotators' labels so that agreement and alternative annotations can be examined directly.
